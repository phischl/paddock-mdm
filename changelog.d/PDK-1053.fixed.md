- A platform administrator can no longer change the domains of an organization that is being deleted or was deleted.
  The change is refused with 409 `invalid_state` and recorded once in the platform audit trail, so no audit or event
  data of the deleted organization is left behind. Re-creating an organization whose earlier attempt failed no longer
  brings it back while its deletion is starting.
- A failed domain change of any organization is now recorded in the platform audit trail, with the organization's ID
  in `target_organization_id`; a successful change is still recorded in the organization.
