- Up to and including 0.1.0-alpha.10, an administrator without a second factor could set one up at sign-in, in the
  portal and in the device sign-in. From this release, administrators set up a second factor only through the one-time
  sign-in setup link. After the upgrade, check in Authentik that every second factor registered for an administrator
  belongs to that administrator, and remove any you do not recognise.
