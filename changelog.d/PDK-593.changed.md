- Evidence exports: the audit writer now spools a package's events on its own disk volume (`audit-writer-tmp`)
  instead of in memory, and an export larger than `PADDOCK_AUDIT_EXPORT_MAX_BYTES` (`paddock-audit-writer`, default
  4 GiB) fails cleanly with the reason `export_too_large` instead of exhausting the audit host. Objects larger than
  256 MiB are no longer read into memory by the export or the chain verification. Make sure the audit host has free
  disk space for at least one export of that size.
