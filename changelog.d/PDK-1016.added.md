- Devices that were switched off during an organization's grace period still remove Paddock when they come back after
  the organization was deleted: at their next contact they receive only two commands, first the disarm of the dead
  man's switch and then the removal, and nothing else (no configuration, agent update or time ticket). Local data and
  disk encryption stay, and the device is never locked or wiped, even when it cannot remove Paddock at once. An
  administrator's own removal that would expire before the device returns is renewed with the same mode. The device's
  report of the removal is recorded in the platform audit log as `platform.offboarded_device_uninstalled`. A device
  that never comes back is forgotten after 365 days (`retention.purged`, store `offboarded_device`). Such devices never
  hold up the deletion of their organization.
