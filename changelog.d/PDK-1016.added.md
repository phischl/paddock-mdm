- Devices that were switched off during an organization's grace period still remove Paddock when they come back after
  the organization was deleted: at their next contact they receive only the removal (mode leave) and nothing else, no
  configuration, agent update or time ticket; local data and disk encryption stay, and the device is never locked or
  wiped. Their report of the removal is recorded in the platform audit log as `platform.offboarded_device_uninstalled`.
  A device that never comes back is forgotten after 365 days (`retention.purged`, store `offboarded_device`). Such
  devices never hold up the deletion of their organization.
