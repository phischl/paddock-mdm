- The platform organization list shows a pending deletion with its date and the deletion steps with their last error,
  filters by the statuses deletion running and deleted, and offers Delete (typed slug, reason, grace period of 7 to 90
  days, step-up) and Cancel deletion. While a deletion is pending, the administrators of the organization see a banner
  with its date and the advice to secure the recovery keys of the encrypted disks and the passwords of the local
  administrators.
- Deleted organizations offer their retained audit log and the signed evidence exports (request with a step-up,
  download) to platform administrators, with the outcome and date of the final verification of the audit chain; a failed
  verification is shown as an error. A device uninstall that an organization's deletion issued is labelled as such on
  the device page and cannot be cancelled there.
