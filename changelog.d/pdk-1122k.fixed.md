- One device's unusual inventory data no longer stops inventory updates and Fleet-based reports for other devices: the
  inventory round skips only that device, logs a warning with its device ID and counts it in the new metric
  `paddock_inventory_hosts_skipped_total`. The organization's audit log and findings show the skipped device with the
  new event `device.tamper_inventory_rejected`, once until its inventory is stored again. Software, vulnerability and
  policy names are limited to the lengths Fleet itself stores.
