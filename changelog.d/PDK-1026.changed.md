- Upgrade: administrators (members of `paddock.platform.admins` or of an organization's `.admins`, `.operators` or
  `.auditors` group) who have no passkey at the upgrade are asked to set one up at their next portal sign-in, before
  they can continue; accounts that already sign in with an authenticator app keep it and add the passkey. An
  administrator added after the upgrade sets up the passkey only through a one-time sign-in setup link. The portal
  announces the change with a banner for 30 days after the upgrade.
- A new administrator account can no longer set up its second factor in the sign-in itself, so knowing its password is
  not enough to take it over. A sign-in without a second factor is refused, and the new administrator sets up the
  password and a passkey through a one-time sign-in setup link that expires after 24 hours. Device users still set
  up their second factor at their first device sign-in.
- The one-time links for new local users now also set up a passkey after the password. The link's page is titled
  "Set up your sign-in". An authenticator app is added in the identity provider's user settings as before.
- Setting and removing the sign-in setup marks is recorded in the audit log (`platform.mfa_enrol_marks_seeded`,
  `platform.mfa_enrol_marks_cleared`).
- A portal sign-in refused because the account has no passkey yet now says so, and explains that the passkey is set up
  through a one-time sign-in setup link.
