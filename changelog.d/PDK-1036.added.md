- While an organization's deletion is pending, its administrator (`POST /api/v1/recovery-export`) or the platform
  administrator (`POST /api/platform/v1/organizations/{id}/recovery-export`) can download every disk recovery key and
  the newest local administrator password of the organization's devices as one OpenPGP file, encrypted to their own
  public key or a passphrase of at least 16 characters; `gpg --decrypt` opens it. The export needs a step-up, is
  limited to five per grace period (409 `export_limit`), is recorded once as `organization.recovery_exported` in the
  organization's audit log without any secret, and is never stored on the server. The portal offers it on the overview
  of the organization administrator and in the platform organization list. Database migration 00133 adds the export
  count.
