- One retention round in `paddock-worker` (ADR 0032, PDK-137) replaces the login history purge: every 6 hours
  (`PADDOCK_RETENTION_INTERVAL`) it purges, per organization, the login history and the users seen on devices whose
  last login is older than 365 days. Each purge that deletes rows is recorded as `retention.purged` with its store
  (`device_login_event`, `device_user_seen`), the cutoff and the number of deleted rows, in the same transaction as the
  deletion; a failed purge rolls back and is recorded with outcome failure. New metrics
  `paddock_retention_last_success_timestamp_seconds{store}`, `paddock_retention_deleted_total{store}` and
  `paddock_retention_stores`, alerts `PaddockRetentionPurgeStale` (critical, a store not purged for 48 hours) and
  `PaddockRetentionPurgeMissing` (warning). Schema: migration `00075` (the worker may delete users seen on devices).
