- Patch SLA and patch compliance report (plan M13c decisions 17 and 18): organizations set how many days a vulnerability with an available fix may stay open per severity (`/settings/patch-sla`, `GET`/`PUT /api/v1/settings/patch-sla`, `paddock.yml` section `settings.patch_sla`; defaults 7/30/90 days, none for low). The new portal page *Patch compliance* (`GET /api/v1/patch-compliance`, export as JSON or CSV) shows the current state: breached findings per severity, device group and device and the devices behind a pinned required application, for administrators, operators and auditors. Devices beyond the SLA appear on the attention list (`patch_sla_breached`), and the worker records a daily `vulnerability.sla_breached` summary that notification destinations with `all` or a custom list receive. New audit codes: `settings.patch_sla_changed`, `patch_compliance.viewed`, `patch_compliance.exported`, `vulnerability.sla_breached`.
