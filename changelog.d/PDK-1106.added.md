- Runbook for an object store that keeps being killed after a large deletion: after a bundle prune or the deletion of
  a large organization, RustFS can load its stale per-bucket usage cache at every start and be OOM-killed in a loop
  although few objects are left. `docs/operations/capacity.md` ("Object store after a large deletion") shows how to
  recognise it, how to move the cache aside inside the volume without deleting anything, and when to do so
  pre-emptively (more than 20 000 deleted objects per GiB of `PADDOCK_RUSTFS_MEM_LIMIT`); the residual risk is RR-147.
