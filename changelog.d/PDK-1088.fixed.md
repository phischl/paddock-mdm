- The cache sync no longer rewrites unchanged entries every minute. Before, after a large organization was deleted,
  rewriting the keys and uninstall commands its devices keep for a year grew Valkey's append-only file by hundreds of
  MB a minute until Valkey was killed at its memory limit, which interrupted check-ins and background work of every
  other organization. The devices of deleted organizations are now restored at most every 15 minutes, in pages of
  1 000, and `paddock_device_cache_reconcile_writes_total{kind}` counts what the reconcile still writes (flat when
  nothing changes).
- Deleting an organization no longer floods the worker's log with one warning per open attention finding and per
  device change notification: the findings round stops at the organization's first refused write, and the cache sync
  logs one line per deleting organization.
