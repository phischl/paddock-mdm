- The host command `admin enrolment-link` also creates the sign-in setup link of an organization's administrator,
  operator or auditor, for example the first administrator of a new organization. For an account created by hand in
  the identity provider the link sets the password and the passkey; an account from the identity source sets up only
  its passkey, and the command says so. Accounts without a portal role are refused. The audit event
  `platform.enrolment_link_created` names the account's organization (`organization_id`).
- Accounts provisioned through SCIM count as accounts from the identity source: their sign-in setup link never sets a
  local password. The Paddock service account may now read Authentik's SCIM source users (read only).
- The installation guide and the getting-started guide describe where an organization's administrators come from and
  how the first one gets their sign-in setup link.
