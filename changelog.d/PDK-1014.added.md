- A platform administrator can request the deletion of an organization with a step-up, its typed slug, a reason and a
  grace period of 7 to 90 days (`POST /api/platform/v1/organizations/{id}/delete`) and cancel it during the grace
  period (`.../cancel-deletion`). The request tells every active device of the organization to remove Paddock
  (uninstall, mode `leave`); the cancel withdraws the uninstalls the devices have not received yet. The organization
  shows the pending deletion, its administrators see its date in `GET /api/v1/me`, and the organization list hides
  deleted organizations unless filtered by status. The data itself is not deleted yet in this step.
- An uninstall issued by an organization's deletion shows the reason `organization_offboarding` on the device
  (`uninstall.reason`); the organization administrator cannot cancel it per device (409 `invalid_state`), only
  cancelling the deletion withdraws it.
