- Deleting an organization now ends the pending sign-in setup links of every member of its groups, including accounts
  synced from an upstream directory or created by hand in the identity provider, and removes their mark that lets a
  setup link set a password; such accounts stay in the identity provider but no longer hold a usable link into the
  deleted organization.
- `paddock-server admin enrolment-link` refuses an account of an organization that is being deleted (`invalid_state`)
  instead of issuing a link that would outlive the organization.
