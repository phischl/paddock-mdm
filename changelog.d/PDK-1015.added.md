- When an organization's grace period ends, the worker deletes it within minutes, one step per minute: it ends the
  administrators' portal and MCP sessions and revokes the API tokens, waits until the organization's audit events are
  published, deletes its Authentik groups, users created by Paddock, device login application, provider, mapping and
  access policy (users synced from upstream stay), its Fleet hosts unless another organization's device uses them, its
  objects, its cached device keys and its database rows, and finally leaves the organization as a tombstone in status
  `deleted`. Devices that have not reported their removal do not hold the deletion: they are handed over and remove
  Paddock at their next contact. The audit evidence stays. The platform audit log records `organization.deletion_step_completed` per step
  and `organization.deleted` with the counts per store.
- A failing step is retried with a growing wait (up to 30 minutes) and continues by itself once its dependency is back;
  the organization shows the step and its error code, `organization.deletion_step_failed` is recorded once per error
  code, and the new alert `PaddockOrganizationDeletionStuck` fires while a step is retried at the longest wait. A
  deletion that waits more than an hour for database transactions that were open when it started records
  `finalize_waiting`. New worker metrics: `paddock_offboarding_step_failing`, `paddock_offboarding_retry_capped`,
  `paddock_offboarding_retry_cap_reached_total`, `paddock_offboarding_finalize_busy_total`.
- Deleting an organization now also removes its sign-in application objects in the identity provider: the Paddock
  service account in the Authentik blueprint may now delete the organization's device login application, provider,
  access policy, its bindings and the groups mapping.
