- The database connection limit is configurable with `PADDOCK_PG_MAX_CONNECTIONS` (default 100) and the number of
  ingest workers with `PADDOCK_WORKER_INGEST_REPLICAS` (default 0), both kept across `./paddock up`, upgrades and
  restores. A restore starts the database with at least the limit its backup was written with, a rollback to
  0.1.0-alpha.10 is refused above 100 (set it back to 100 and run the rollback again), and `./paddock check` reports
  the connection budget of the configured roles and replicas against the limit less the connections kept for the
  superuser, pgBackRest, migrations and one operator command, failing when it exceeds it.
