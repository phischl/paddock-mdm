- The worker's retention round (ADR 0032, PDK-138) deletes the inventory of retired devices: once a device has been
  retired for the organization's `retired_inventory_days` (privacy settings, default 30), its software, vulnerability
  findings, policy results and hardware facts are deleted in Paddock and its host is deleted in Fleet, at most 100
  devices per organization and round. Each device is recorded as one `device.inventory_purged` event with the deleted
  counts and whether the Fleet host was deleted. While Fleet is unavailable the event has outcome failure
  (`upstream_unavailable`), nothing is deleted in Paddock and the device is retried in the next round; a Fleet host
  that an active device of the same hardware uses again is kept. `paddock_retention_stores` is now 3 (new store
  `retired_inventory`).
