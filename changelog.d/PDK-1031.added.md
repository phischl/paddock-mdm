- Additional ingest workers for high device-report volumes: the new Compose service `paddock-worker-ingest` (no replicas
  by default, `PADDOCK_WORKER_INGEST_REPLICAS=N` in `.env`) runs a worker with `PADDOCK_WORKER_MODE=ingest` that only processes
  device reports, enrollments, command results and escrow uploads, holds at most 11 instead of 30 database connections
  without the Authentik, Fleet or bundle-bucket credentials, and reports `ready mode=ingest` on its readiness endpoint.
  Two of them need a raised `PADDOCK_PG_MAX_CONNECTIONS` (docs/operations/capacity.md, "PostgreSQL connection budget").
