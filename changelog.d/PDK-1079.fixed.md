- Queries of a small organization no longer slow down or time out those of a large one on the same server: the
  attention list and count, the findings round, the outdated-schema and config rollout device lists and the event-gap
  checks of an organization with tens of thousands of devices could run for seconds to minutes after the same
  connection had served a small organization. Every role that serves organizations now plans their queries for the
  organization it serves, at a cost of about 0.1 ms per query, and runs them without just-in-time compilation, which
  cost about 2 s per attention query at 70 000 devices. Check-in and event processing still reuses the plans of its
  inserts and of its device-keyed event-table queries, which new device indexes on the event tables keep fast in
  organizations of every size; its device and status lookups are planned for the organization they serve.
