- Before a deleted organization's records are removed, its audit log is verified one last time and then closed: the
  log ends with `audit.chain_verified` (or `audit.chain_verification_failed`) with `final: true`, the last day is
  sealed with the mark `retired`, the nightly verification no longer checks it, and an event that arrives later is
  kept in the platform audit log as `audit.event_after_retirement` instead of being lost. The deletion waits for this
  step and shows its outcome on the organization (`audit_final_outcome`) and in `organization.deleted`; a failed final
  verification does not stop the deletion and raises the new alert `PaddockAuditFinalVerificationFailed`. The control
  plane never connects to the audit database for this: the audit writer reports the outcome on a new message channel
  that only it may publish to (RabbitMQ exchange `paddock.offboarding`, queue `worker.offboarding`; the broker applies
  the new permissions at its next start).
- Platform administrators can read the retained audit log of a deleted organization and produce its evidence export
  for the organization's auditors (`/api/platform/v1/organizations/{id}/audit-events` and `.../audit-exports`, the
  export with a step-up). The package verifies offline with `paddockctl audit verify-export`. Every read is recorded in
  the platform audit log as `platform.deleted_organization_audit_read`; organizations that are not deleted, and every
  caller who is not a platform administrator, get 404.
- `paddock-server audit deletions --since <RFC3339>` lists every `organization.deleted` since that moment from the
  audit bucket, so that the deletions can be repeated after restoring a backup taken before them.
