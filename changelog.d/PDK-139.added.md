- Read-access recording (ADR 0032 §4, PDK-139): while an organization switches it on in its privacy settings, a
  search of the audit log (`GET /api/v1/audit-events`, also through the MCP and with API tokens) is recorded as
  `audit.searched` once per actor and clock hour, with its filters and whether a search text was used but never the
  text, and a view of a device's software or vulnerabilities as `device.inventory_viewed` once per actor, device and
  UTC day. A role that may not read the audit log is recorded as denied. The coalescing keys are kept 48 hours; the
  worker's retention round deletes older ones as a fourth pass with a `retention.purged` event (store
  `read_audit_window`), and `paddock_retention_stores` is now 4. Schema: migration `00079`.
