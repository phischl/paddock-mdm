- Operators get a runbook for deleting an organization (`docs/operations/organization-offboarding.md`): request and
  grace period, the encrypted recovery export to take before the deletion, what users and offline devices experience,
  what to do for every failure of the deletion, and how to repeat a deletion after a restore to an earlier point. The
  retention schedule, privacy notes, residual risks (RR-137, RR-138, RR-140, RR-142, RR-143) and the ISO 27001 mapping (A.8.10)
  describe what an offboarding deletes and what it keeps.
