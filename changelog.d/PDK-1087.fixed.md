- Deleting a large organization no longer holds up the deletion of other organizations. Every step whose work grows
  with the organization (handing over devices, Fleet hosts, stored objects, cached keys, database rows) now works in
  short slices and lets the other deletions continue in between; stored objects are removed up to 1 000 per request
  instead of one by one. A small organization next to one with tens of thousands of devices is again deleted within
  minutes. A failed step is retried only after its backoff counted from the end of the failed attempt, a deletion that
  waits for the audit writer no longer spins the worker, and the same failure code is recorded in the audit log once
  per deletion. The new alert `PaddockOrganizationDeletionRoundOverrun` warns when a deletion round runs longer than
  2 minutes.
