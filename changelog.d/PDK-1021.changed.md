- The worker now checks an organization's identity provider objects (groups, device login application) in full only
  after a change: when the organization is created, its domains change, a round reports an identity provider error,
  or a new Paddock version changes those objects, and otherwise every 6 to 7 hours. The ten-minute reconcile round
  in between only aligns the per-device login groups, which cuts the identity provider requests of that round by
  about 90 % (more with many organizations). A group deleted by hand in the identity provider is recreated at the
  latest after 7 hours, or at once after a domain change of its organization.
